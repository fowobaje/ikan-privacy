Privacy Policy for IKAN

Effective Date: November 22, 2026

IKAN ("we," "our," or "us") provides offline-first invoicing, receipt generation, waybill management, and bookkeeping tools for businesses and independent professionals. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you use the IKAN mobile application.

1. Information We Collect and Process

  • Account Information: If you create an account or enable cloud backup/sync, we collect your name, email address, and authentication identifiers via Google Firebase Authentication.
  • Business & Financial Records: Business profile details (company name, address, phone number, tax ID, logo, digital signature), client contacts, line items, custom tax rates, invoices, receipts, waybills, and bookkeeping entries you enter into the app.
  • Camera Permission (Barcode & QR Scanning): IKAN requests access to your device camera solely to scan 1D barcodes and QR codes when adding items to documents. Camera frames are processed locally on your device in real time and are never recorded, stored, or transmitted to external servers.
  • Local Storage & Backups: By default, your records are stored locally on your device in an encrypted SQLite/Room database. You may also export manual backup files (.ikanbackup) or PDFs directly to your device storage.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To generate invoices, receipts, waybills, and financial summaries.
  • To synchronize your business workspace across authorized devices when cloud sync is enabled.
  • To authenticate your account and protect against unauthorized access.

3. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or business financial data to third parties. When you use cloud authentication or cloud synchronization, data is processed securely using Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore), encrypted in transit via HTTPS/TLS.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Local data remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. If you use cloud sync, you may request complete deletion of your account and associated cloud records at any time by contacting us at the email below or using the account deletion option within the app settings.

5. Children's Privacy

IKAN is designed for business and commercial use by individuals aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be reflected on this page with a revised effective date.
